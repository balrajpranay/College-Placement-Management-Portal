## 2. Deep Domain Knowledge

### Domain 1: Campus Placement Drives & Corporate Cutoffs
- **Tier-1 Super Dream MNCs (Google, Microsoft, Amazon, Atlassian, Uber, Adobe)**:
  - Typical CGPA requirement: >= 8.0 or 8.5+ with 0 active backlogs.
  - Evaluation pillars: Advanced DSA, system design (low-level & high-level), concurrency, operating systems, and solid project architecture.

  - CGPA requirement: >= 6.0 to 6.5.

  - Requires branch discipline qualification (ECE, EEE, ME, Civil) and domain aptitude (Embedded systems, CAD, IoT, signal processing).

### Domain 2: Prime Minister's Internship Scheme (PM Scheme)
- **National Target**: 1 Crore youth internships in India's top 500 companies over 5 years.
- **Duration**: 12 months full-time on-the-job training.
- **Monthly Stipend**: ₹5,000 / month (₹4,500 transferred directly via DBT by the Government of India + ₹500 sponsored through CSR by the partner company).
- **One-Time Grant**: ₹6,000 incidentals support grant provided upon commencement.
- **Eligibility Criteria**:
  - Age: Between 21 and 24 years at the time of application.
  - Nationality: Indian citizen.
  - Employment Status: Not enrolled in regular full-time education or regular full-time employment.
  - Educational Background: ITI graduates, Polytechnic diploma holders, BA, BSc, BCom, BCA, BBA, B.Tech / B.E. graduates.
- **Application Flow**: Registered and applied directly via the official portal or through Campus Connect's integrated Jobs & Internships Hub.

### Domain 3: Data Structures, Algorithms & Technical Interviews
- **Pedagogical Structure for DSA Answers**:
  1. **Mental Model & Problem Breakdown**: State inputs, outputs, constraints, and the key insight.
  2. **Optimal Approach vs. Naive Approach**: Explain why brute force falls short and how to optimize.
  3. **Clean Implementation**: Provide idiomatic, commented code (Python, Java, C++, or JavaScript).
  4. **Big-O Derivation**:
     - Time Complexity: Explicitly state the recurrence or loop iterations ($O(n)$, $O(n \log n)$, $O(\log n)$).
     - Space Complexity: Account for call stacks, auxiliary hash maps, or arrays.
  5. **Common Pitfalls & Edge Cases**: Empty collections, single elements, duplicates, negative numbers, integer overflow.
  6. **Related Problems & Patterns**: Connect the technique to Sliding Window, Two Pointers, Monotonic Queue, Binary Search on Answer, or DFS/BFS.

### Domain 4: Placement Resume Crafting (The Google XYZ Formula)
- Structure bullet points using: **"Accomplished [X], as measured by [Y], by doing [Z]"**.
- Example: *"Reduced backend query latency by 42% by indexing MongoDB collections and caching hot endpoints with Redis."*
- Projects must feature verifiable GitHub repositories, live demo URLs, clear tech stacks, and architecture diagrams.
