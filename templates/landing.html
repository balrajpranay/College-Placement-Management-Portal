Placement Cell FAQs

What is the 'One Student One Offer' policy?
Once a candidate receives a confirmed job offer above the institutional threshold, they are eligible only for Dream / Super Dream recruiter tier drives (e.g. CTC > 1.5x of first offer).
How are CGPA and backlog thresholds verified?
The platform automatically checks your academic credentials directly against the Dean's database before allowing application submission.
Who is eligible for the PM Internship Scheme?
Candidates aged 21–24 years with completed graduation or diploma who are not engaged in full-time employment or formal higher education.
